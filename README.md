# epidemic-mean-field-topics

Code and aggregated data for modelling the propagation of research topics in co-authorship networks with epidemiological models (SI, SIS and SIR, under homogeneous mixing and the degree-block approximation).

## Where to find the code and data

The code and the aggregated data are archived on Zenodo:

**https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23128970**

To reproduce the results, download the archive from Zenodo and follow its `README.md`.

## Related publications

- Cruz-Trani, J., Robles-Martínez, I., Aguirre-Guerrero, D., Marcelín-Jiménez, R.: Dynamic Modeling of Topic Propagation in Coauthorship Networks Using Epidemiological Models. In: Proceedings of The Sixth International Symposium on Complex Systems (ISCS 2026), Studies in Systems, Decision and Control, vol. 709, Springer (2027). https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-39646-4_17
- Robles-Martínez, I., Cruz-Trani, J., Aguirre-Guerrero, D., Marcelín-Jiménez, R.: Epidemiological Models of Topic Propagation in Co-authorship Networks: Degree-Block Models Versus Homogeneous Mixing. Submitted to Applied Network Science (2026).

## Data

The bibliographic records were obtained from Scopus (Elsevier) under an institutional subscription and cannot be redistributed. The Zenodo archive contains only aggregated data derived from them (yearly series and counts per topic, year and degree class).
